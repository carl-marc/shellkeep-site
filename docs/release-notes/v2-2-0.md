**Released:** 6 August 2026

## v2.2.0 (126)

### Improvements

- Added the new collection key to the marketplace, selling, fusing, merging and collection screens
- Cards screen: added a divider between egg rarities
- Profiles: you can now see and view a player's club

### Bug fixes

- Merging cards sometimes didn't update the stock counts on the details page (display issue only)
- Completing a Full Egg Collection with a card won at auction or bought from Daily Deals now grants the achievement reward straight away, instead of waiting for the next card you create, sell, merge or fuse
