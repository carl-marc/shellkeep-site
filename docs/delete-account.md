# Delete Account

## How to delete your account

1. Open Shellkeep
2. Go to **Settings**
3. Tap **Delete Account**
4. Confirm the deletion

Your account will be scheduled for deletion with a **3-day grace period**. If you sign back in within 3 days, your account will be restored.

After 3 days, your account and all associated data will be **permanently deleted**.

## What data is deleted

- Player profile and username
- Authentication credentials
- Club membership
- Inbox and social data

## What data is retained

Some gameplay data (such as card and auction records) may be retained in an anonymised, orphaned state with no link to your identity. This data cannot be used to identify you after account deletion.

## Need help?

If you are unable to access the app and need your account deleted, contact **support@hamlab.dev** with the email address associated with your account.
