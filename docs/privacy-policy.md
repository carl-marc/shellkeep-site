# Privacy Policy
**Last updated:** {{ "2026-05-02" | date: "%d %b %Y" }}

Your privacy is important to us. It is Hamlab's policy to respect your privacy regarding any information we may collect from you through the Shellkeep app.


## Information We Collect

When you use Shellkeep, we may collect the following:

- **Account information:** Your email address and display name when you sign in via Google Sign-In or Apple Sign-In.
- **Game data:** Game progress, statistics, cards, and other gameplay data stored in our database.
- **Device tokens:** Push notification tokens (Firebase Cloud Messaging) to send you game notifications. You can disable notifications in your device settings at any time.
- **Analytics data:** We use Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous usage data such as app opens, screen views, and in-game events to help us understand how players use the app and improve the experience.


## How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

- Provide and maintain the game service
- Enable social features (friends, clubs, marketplace)
- Send push notifications about game events
- Understand how players use the app and improve the experience
- Prevent abuse and enforce our terms


## Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase infrastructure. We protect your information within commercially acceptable means to prevent loss, theft, and unauthorised access.

We only retain collected information for as long as necessary to provide the service. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.


## Third-Party Services

Shellkeep uses Google Firebase for authentication, data storage, notifications, and analytics. These services are governed by [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).


## Data Deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data from within the app at any time (Profile → Delete Account). Upon deletion, your personal data will be permanently removed from our systems within 30 days.


## Children's Privacy

Shellkeep is suitable for all ages. Account creation requires signing in with Google or Apple, which requires parental involvement for young users. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without parental consent. If you are a parent and believe your child's data has been collected without your consent, please contact us and we will promptly remove it.


## Your Rights

You are free to:

- Request a copy of your data
- Delete your account and data at any time


## External Links

The app may link to external sites not operated by us. We have no control over their content or privacy practices.


## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.


## Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy or how we handle your data, contact us at **support@hamlab.dev**.
